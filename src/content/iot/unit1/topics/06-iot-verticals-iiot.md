# IoT Verticals & Industrial IoT

## Horizontal platforms vs vertical domains

> [!NOTE]
> An **IoT vertical** is an *industry-specific* domain where IoT is applied to solve that industry's unique problems. A **horizontal platform** provides the *cross-industry* foundation — cloud services, data analytics, security, connectivity — reused by every vertical.

> [!INTUITION]
> Think of a **horizontal** as the shared *plumbing* (cloud, analytics, security) and a **vertical** as the *building* plumbed for one purpose (a hospital vs a factory). The same analytics engine (horizontal) powers both predictive-maintenance in a factory and patient-monitoring in a hospital (verticals). Verticals are where **real-world impact and revenue** are created.

## Industrial IoT (IIoT) — the backbone of Industry 4.0

**IIoT** integrates **sensors, machines, and analytics platforms** across manufacturing, oil & gas, mining, and utilities. It is considered the **backbone of Industry 4.0** (the fourth industrial revolution — cyber-physical, data-driven factories).

### Signature IIoT use cases

- **Predictive maintenance** — vibration and thermal sensors detect a failing bearing *before* it breaks, so you repair on schedule instead of after a costly breakdown.
- **Asset tracking & uptime monitoring** — know where every machine and tool is and whether it's running.
- **Digital-twin modelling** — a live virtual replica of a process or machine for simulation and optimisation.

> [!INTUITION]
> The killer app of IIoT is **predictive maintenance**. *Reactive* maintenance fixes things after they break (expensive downtime). *Preventive* maintenance services on a fixed calendar (often too early or too late). **Predictive** maintenance uses live sensor data to fix things *exactly when needed* — the sweet spot that saves the most money.

### IIoT applications by industry

| Industry | Representative applications |
|---|---|
| **Manufacturing** | Real-time monitoring · predictive maintenance · automated quality assurance (instant defect detection) |
| **Agriculture** | Precision farming · automated irrigation · livestock monitoring |
| **Energy** | Smart grids (balance supply/demand) · renewable monitoring · early fault detection |
| **Transport & logistics** | Fleet management · smart warehousing (RFID inventory) · cargo condition tracking |

### Benefits of IIoT

- Increases **efficiency and productivity**.
- Creates **new business opportunities**.
- **Reduces asset lifecycle cost**.
- Enhances **worker safety** (remote monitoring of hazardous environments).
- Drives **product innovation**.
- Improves understanding of **consumer demand**.

> [!EXAM]
> Common questions: **define a vertical vs a horizontal platform**, **list 4–5 verticals**, and for **IIoT** — state that it is the *backbone of Industry 4.0*, name its three flagship use cases (**predictive maintenance, asset tracking, digital twins**), and explain why **predictive** beats **reactive/preventive** maintenance.
